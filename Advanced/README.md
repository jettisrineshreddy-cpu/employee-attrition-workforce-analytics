# Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed as part of the **ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship — Advanced Level**.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce patterns using employee-level HR data. The dashboard is designed as an HR decision-support tool that helps identify where attrition is concentrated and which workforce segments require closer attention.

The analysis focuses on department, job role, overtime, monthly income, tenure, age group, and job satisfaction.

## Objectives

* Measure overall employee attrition.
* Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
* Examine workforce factors associated with employee attrition.
* Provide interactive filtering for HR analysis.
* Translate analytical findings into practical retention recommendations.

## Key Insights

### Sales Representative Risk

Sales has a **20.63%** attrition rate, while Sales Representatives reach **39.76%**, indicating that the risk is particularly concentrated in this role.

### Overtime Risk

Employees working overtime have a **30.53%** attrition rate compared with **10.44%** for employees who do not work overtime.

### Early-Career Risk

Employees with **0–1 year** of tenure have a **34.88%** attrition rate, while employees under 25 reach **39.18%**.

### Compensation & Satisfaction

Employees earning below **₹3K** have a **28.61%** attrition rate, while the lowest job-satisfaction group has a **22.84%** attrition rate.

## Recommendations

* Target retention efforts toward Sales Representatives.
* Monitor overtime, workload distribution, and staffing.
* Strengthen first-year onboarding, mentoring, and career support.
* Review the lowest compensation band with targeted retention actions.

## Analytical Note

The dashboard identifies **associations and risk signals**, not proven causal relationships. For example, higher overtime is associated with higher attrition, but the analysis does not establish that overtime directly causes employees to leave.
